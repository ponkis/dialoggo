Getting Started

Dialoggo recreates Banjo-Kazooie style dialogue boxes. Type your line, pick a character, pick a background, and press play. The preview box above renders the scene exactly as it will export.

  1. 1Write your dialogue in the text field.
  2. 2Pick a character from the film reel.
  3. 3(Optional) Open the Backgrounds sleeve and choose a backdrop.
  4. 4Hit Play. Stop or pause any time.
Playback
Play Starts typing out the dialogue from the beginning.
Stop Ends playback and clears the dialogue box.
Pause Freezes the line mid-type. Press Play to resume.
Fast Forward Makes the overall dialogue go faster.
Keyboard Shortcuts
  • Space Play / Pause Toggles the current playback state.
  • Ctrl+Space Stop Ends playback instantly.
  • Shift+Space Fast Forward Makes dialogue go faster.
  • Esc Clear Search Empties the character search field while focused.

Enable Tooltip visualizer in Settings to see shortcut hints float over the button you're about to press.

Dialogue Modifiers

Wrap a piece of text with these markers to style it inside the dialogue box. A small toolbar also appears above the text field when you highlight something.

  • **text** Shaky Letters vibrate while typing. Great for tense or loud lines.
  • _text_ Italic Slants the text for emphasis or inner thoughts.
  • ~~text~~ Strike Crosses the text out, as if the speaker changed their mind.
Characters & Backgrounds
  • Film reel. Scroll horizontally through every available character. The arrows on either side jump one page at a time.
  • Search. Start typing to filter characters by name. Esc clears it.
  • Upload. The + button lets you bring your own character sprite into the reel.
  • Backgrounds sleeve. Open it from the blue tab. Browse packs, pick a backdrop, and it loads behind the dialogue.
Favorites

Right-click any character card or background thumbnail to open a small context menu and add it to your favorites. Favorites surface to the top of the list so you can find them fast.

Settings Explained
Mirror mode
Flips the dialogue box. Regular starts on the left side of the screen, mirrored starts on the right — useful when the speaker should face the other way.
N64 mode
Downsamples the dialogue rendering to a chunky, low-resolution look reminiscent of Nintendo 64 games.
Tooltip visualizer
Floats a small overlay showing which keys are held and which action is about to fire. Also flashes a little badge when you favorite something.
Hide broken characters
Hides any character that's missing assets so the reel stays tidy.
Hide broken backgrounds
Same idea for backgrounds — anything that can't load is filtered out of the pack.
Tips
  • Mix multiple modifiers in one line — **loud and _emphatic_** works.
  • Favorites persist between sessions. Clear them by right-clicking again.
  • The camera tab is reserved for exporting. It's disabled while a line is playing.
