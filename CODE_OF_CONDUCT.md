# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Dialoggo spaces to be respectful, useful, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Treat others with patience, assume good intent where reasonable, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

## Our standards

Examples of helpful behavior include asking clear questions, explaining disagreements constructively, and accepting feedback gracefully. Harassment, insults, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

This standard applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Dialoggo project.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. To report a conduct concern privately, contact the project maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/ponkis).
